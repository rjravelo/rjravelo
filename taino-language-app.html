— Rosemary J. Ravelo · UX Research Insights

Strategic UX Recommendations

  • Active Speaking Quests: Replaced passive reading exercises with real-world contextual speaking prompts.
  • Milestone Feedback Loops: Designed visible achievement nodes to satisfy intrinsic goal-completion motives (66.7%).
  • End-Game Retention Loop: Implemented daily practice challenges to prevent skill decay.

Research Expansion & Next Steps

  • Knowledge Repository: Established a standardized research asset library for future language preservation initiatives.
  • Alpha Usability Testing: Conducted rapid feedback loops with community native language preservationists.
