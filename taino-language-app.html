Taino Language App UX Research Insights – Taíno Learning Styles

Objective

Explore effective learning styles and motivations to inform the Taíno Language Project’s design decisions.

Key Findings

Preferred Learning Formats
  • Speaking practice (72.2%)
  • Games or challenges (72.2%)
  • Social interaction (55.6%)
Tracking Progress Preferences
  • Milestone tracking with visual goal indicators (83%)
  • Constructive feedback (72%)
  • Tests and quizzes (67%)
Top Motivations for Learning
  • Practical application of knowledge (72.2%)
  • Achievable goal-setting (66.7%)
  • Earning rewards (66.7%)

Recommendations

  • Prioritize Speaking: Add speaking exercises and implement audio playback by Taíno speakers.
  • Modern Context: Introduce real-world scenarios (e.g., ordering food) and track conversational milestones.
  • Gamification: Integrate games and challenges to increase engagement.

Additional Initiatives

  • Research Repository: Created a shared repository on collectivist learning methods to support cross-team collaboration.
  • Rapid Usability Testing: Conducted quick usability tests to validate the working prototype’s core functionality.
