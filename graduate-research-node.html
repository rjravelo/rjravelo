OBJECTIVE

Investigate how specific usability features in sandbox environments (Minecraft: Creative Mode) influence player states of flow, immersion, and temporal distortion.

AUDIOVISUAL IMPACT
  • Ambient Sound (90%+ Enh.)
  • Background Music Loops
  • Menu Design Cohesion
FLOW FACILITATORS
  • Hotbar Clarity & UI
  • Inventory Management
  • Social Chat Sync (r=0.71)
TEMPORAL DISTORTION
  • Building & Creative Tasks
  • Goal-Directed Loops
  • Perception of Time Shift
DESIGN IMPLICATIONS
  • Holistic Usability: Audio and social features are core to immersion, not just secondary.
  • Cognitive Load: Intuitive UI reduces friction, allowing faster entry into flow states.
  • Creative Loops: Building tasks are the primary drivers of altered time perception.
FUTURE RESEARCH NODES

FINDINGS SUMMARY

> DISSERTATION_RESULTS.TXT EXTRACT:

The findings suggest that game usability extends beyond traditional definitions. It includes audio design, social connectivity, and creative tasks that—when well-designed—enhance conditions for deeper engagement. Specifically, the bidirectional relationship between flow and immersion creates a positive feedback loop, where achievement in one reinforces the other, ultimately leading to temporal distortion.
